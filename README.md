# 口 kǒu

一个字，被一张张嘴拼读出来。\
A word, spelled out by mouth after mouth.

## 述 shù

拼音通过拉丁字母转写普通话的声音。然而，熟悉的字母并不意味着熟悉的发音。

语言常被想象成一座桥梁，而每一座桥梁，都必然将某些事物遗落在彼岸。没有一种语言系统能够完整地容纳另一种。字母无法承载声音的全部，翻译也无法保留词语所负载的所有重量。意义在流动，也在流失。

这个交互式网站探索文字、发音、身体与意义之间不稳定的关系。通过汉字、拼音、英语与嘴部影像之间的转换，作品呈现第二语言使用者的经验：嘴巴在学习一种语言时，仍然记忆着另一种。它重复、迟疑、出错，在表达与理解之间不断寻找位置。

借鉴爱德华·格里桑的“不透明性”理论，项目试图追问：交流是否必须以完整的理解为前提？

或许，语言将我们连接，并非因为一切都变得透明，而恰恰是因为那些始终无法被翻译的部分。

## Statement

Pinyin writes the sounds of Mandarin in Latin letters. Yet familiar letters do not mean familiar sounds.

Language is often imagined as a bridge, and every bridge must leave something behind on the far shore. No language can wholly contain another. Letters cannot carry the whole of a sound, and translation cannot keep all the weight a word bears. Meaning flows, and meaning slips away.

This interactive site explores the unstable relationship between writing, sound, body and meaning. Moving between Chinese characters, pinyin, English and footage of mouths, the work gives form to the experience of a second-language speaker: a mouth learning one language while still remembering another. It repeats, hesitates, makes mistakes, always searching for its place between expression and understanding.

Drawing on Édouard Glissant’s idea of opacity, the project asks: must communication rest on complete understanding?

Perhaps language connects us not because everything becomes transparent, but precisely because of what can never be translated.
